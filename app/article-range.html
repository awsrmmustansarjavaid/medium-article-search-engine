How It Works

The application first creates a chronological archive. The oldest article becomes #1 and the newest becomes #N. Search, filtering and sorting are performed afterwards.

Permanent Numbering

Article numbers represent their position in the chronological archive. Filtering or sorting never renumbers an article.

Archive Data

A complete articles.json archive is the most reliable source for historical numbering. RSS is only a fallback and may not expose an author's complete history.
